GAS LAW

Charles's Law

V₁ / T₁ = V₂ / T₂

At constant pressure, volume is proportional to absolute temperature.

THE COMMON FRAMEWORK

First Law of Thermodynamics

ΔU = Q − W

Energy enters an ideal gas as heat and leaves when the gas does work. Internal energy follows temperature.

+Q ↓GAS↓ +W

+Q: heat entering. +W: gas expanding and doing work.

State function vs path

For an ideal gas, ΔU depends only on the initial and final temperatures. Different P–V paths can have different Q and W—but the same ΔU.

Path A: W = 260 J, Q = 510 J
Path B: W = 170 J, Q = 420 J
Both: ΔU = +250 J
